The High Cost of a "DIY" Brand Name

Choosing a name via a quick brainstorm or a basic online generator is a gamble. Here is what happens when companies skip professional naming strategy.

⚖️

Trademark Lawsuits

Descriptive or poorly vetted names often infringe on existing trademarks. Rebranding post-launch costs 10x to 100x more than getting it right the first time.

📉

Weak Brand Equity

Clunky, hard-to-spell, or overly literal names fail the "Radio Test," causing you to bleed 15-20% of your direct traffic and marketing spend to typos.

🚧

Acquisition Roadblocks

Enterprise buyers and top-tier VCs scrutinize your digital real estate. A squatted or unprofessional domain signals a lack of long-term strategic vision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this just an AI name generator?
No. While we use advanced AI to expand the creative funnel, our consulting service is defined by human expertise. Our linguists and brand strategists manually curate the results, apply the "Radio Test," conduct preliminary trademark sweeps, and provide strategic context that no software can replicate.
Do you guarantee the name will be approved by the USPTO?
We conduct a thorough preliminary screening of the USPTO database to eliminate high-risk names. However, final trademark approval is always at the discretion of the USPTO examining attorney. We highly recommend engaging a qualified trademark attorney for the final filing, and we can provide referrals.
What if the perfect .com domain is already taken?
This is common for premium names. As part of our service, we will identify if the domain is parked, actively used, or held by an investor. We can then provide a discreet, professional outreach strategy (or act as your proxy broker) to negotiate its acquisition at fair market wholesale/retail value.
